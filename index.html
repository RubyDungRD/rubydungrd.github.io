A modern rebuild of a classic foundation.

RubyDung is an independent open-source project descended from Minecraft 1.5.2, rebuilding its technology, architecture, tooling, and engine piece by piece into something new.

It is not a mod, a server plugin, or a compatibility layer. RubyDung is its own codebase with its own future, guided by everything the Minecraft community has learned over the past seventeen years.

Project Goals

  • Modernize Minecraft 1.5.2 from the ground up.
  • Replace legacy tooling with a modern development workflow.
  • Gradually migrate the project to Kotlin.
  • Modernize rendering and engine architecture.
  • Create an integrated modding API inspired by the best ideas from the community.
  • Eventually replace original assets with original artwork.
